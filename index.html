Help

Questions, answered.

Can't find it here? Our assistant is in the corner of the page, any time of day.

Is my money protected?

Eligible deposits are protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme up to the applicable limit. Your funds are held securely and we're authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

How long does it take to open an account?

Most people finish in under three minutes. You'll need a photo ID and a phone with a camera; verification is usually instant.

Are there any monthly fees?

Our current account has no monthly fee. Any charges that apply to specific products are shown clearly before you apply.

Can I speak to a real person?

Always. Our assistant handles quick questions around the clock and hands over to a specialist whenever a human judgement matters — including for mortgages and investments.

Will checking my rate affect my credit score?

No. We use a soft search to show your personalised rate. A hard search only happens if you choose to go ahead with an application.
